YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy.

Website privacy information · Please review before launch

This first-version website collects the details you submit in an enquiry form: your name, mobile number, the course you are interested in and, if you choose to share it, your city. Enquiries are stored for Edusaaathi to review and respond to.

How information is used

Information submitted through the form is used to understand your question and contact you about your enquiry. Do not include sensitive personal information in the form.

Storage and access

In this starter implementation, enquiry records are stored in the website's server-side data store and are visible to authenticated administrators. Before production, Edusaaathi should confirm its hosting, retention, access-control and deletion processes.

Third-party links

WhatsApp links open a third-party service. If you choose to continue there, that service's own privacy terms apply. A WhatsApp business number must be configured by Edusaaathi before launch.

Questions or requests

For questions about an enquiry or a request to update or remove submitted information, please contact Edusaaathi through the contact details published by the business.

This starter privacy notice is a working draft, not legal advice. Edusaaathi should have it reviewed and replace the operational details above with its final data-handling practices before launch.
